About the Challenge

The Phoenix Phearsome Phour is a self-supported summit challenge built around four of Phoenix's signature mountains: Camelback Mountain, Piestewa Peak, North Mountain, and Shaw Butte. The goal is simple to state and hard to finish — summit as many of the four as you can within a single 24-hour window.

How It Works

  1. Pick your target tier before you start — see Tiers.
  2. Start your 24-hour clock at your first trailhead.
  3. Summit your peaks in any order, by any legal route — see Routes.
  4. Log your times and submit your result — see How to Submit.

Ground Rules

  • Self-supported: you're responsible for your own transport between trailheads.
  • The 24-hour clock starts when you leave your first trailhead and stops when you summit your last peak.
  • All four mountains are public trails — follow posted hours, closures, and Leave No Trace.
  • A GPX file of your route is required as proof of summit.
