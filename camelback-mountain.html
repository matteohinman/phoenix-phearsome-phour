Open in Google Maps

Getting There

Camelback Mountain has two trailheads. Either one works.

Rules

  • Echo Canyon Trailhead opens around 5am and closes at sunset.
  • Cholla Trailhead opens at sunrise and closes at sunset.
  • Sunset is a hard close on Camelback Mountain. No exceptions.
  • Parking is limited and not permitted along most roadways near the trailheads. Vehicles parked in violation of posted signs will be towed at the owner's expense, and idling along the roadways will get you a ticket.

Source: City of Phoenix Parks & Recreation
