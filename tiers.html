Tiers

Four standard tiers, measured by how many times you summit each of the four Phoenix peaks between 5am and 11pm, plus the Ultra, a class of its own.

1 Summit per Peak

Uno Fresco

The entry tier. Summit each of the four peaks once, same day.

 2 Summits per Peak

Dos Caliente

Two summits of every peak, one clock. Things start heating up.

 3 Summits per Peak

Tres Locos

Three summits of every peak between 5am and 11pm. Legs and logistics both get tested.

 4 Summits per Peak

El Cuatro

Four summits of every peak, in any order, between 5am and 11pm.

The Ultra

All 4 Peaks · ~70K Linked

Phoenix Phearsome Phour 70K

The full send: all four summits connected into one continuous 70.4 km route, completed between 5am and 11pm.
